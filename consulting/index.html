02

How we engage

Four shapes. Most projects start as one and grow into another.

1-2 WEEKS

SPIKE

A hard question, answered. Research, a working prototype, and a written recommendation you can act on.

4-12 WEEKS

BUILD

Zero to shipped. We scope it, build it in a shared repo, and hand you the keys.

1-3 WEEKS

AUDIT

Code, architecture, or security review. You get a ranked list of what to fix and why.

ONGOING

RETAINER

A few hours a week of senior judgment. Design reviews, pairing, and help hiring your own team.

Are we a fit

We say no more often than yes. It keeps the work good.

WE LIKE

  • Problems that are hard, not just big
  • Teams that want to understand what we build, not just receive it
  • Open source, or a willingness to open parts of it
  • A technical owner on your side who will argue with us
  • Work we can write about afterwards, with your OK

WE PASS ON

  • Staff augmentation and seat filling
  • Surveillance, dark patterns, and ad-tech
  • Token launches and speculation
  • Fixed bids on a scope nobody has written down
  • Rescues where the deadline was yesterday
